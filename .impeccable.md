## Design Context

### Users
Small SMEs (Small and Medium-sized Enterprises) looking for simple and easy ERP tools. Their context typically involves busy day-to-day operations where they need tools that reduce, rather than add to, their cognitive load.

### Brand Personality
Friendly, welcoming, and grounded. The brand should explicitly avoid feeling too "corporate-ey" or rigid, instead aiming for a warm, cozy, and approachable essence.

### Aesthetic Direction
An Autumn Warm theme paired with a minimalistic layout. The UI should leverage cozy, autumnal colors (e.g., warm olives, soft creams, muted oranges, and subtle browns) while keeping the structure clean and uncluttered. It should support both light and dark modes gracefully.

### Design Principles
1. **Friendly over Corporate**: Use soft styling, comfortable spacing, and warm tones to create an inviting atmosphere devoid of traditional enterprise coldness.
2. **Clarity through Minimalism**: Keep the interface clean and straightforward so that SMEs can manage their ERP tasks easily without feeling overwhelmed.
3. **Autumn Warmth**: Ground the visual experience in an autumnal color palette that evokes a sense of coziness.
4. **Inclusive and Accessible**: Ensure the design natively respects WCAG guidelines, color blindness considerations, and reduced-motion preferences.
