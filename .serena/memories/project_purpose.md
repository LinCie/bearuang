# BearUang Project Purpose

BearUang is an ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) tool designed for Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs). 
The goal is to provide a simple, easy-to-use interface that reduces cognitive load for busy business owners.

## Brand Personality
Friendly, welcoming, and grounded. It avoids the traditional "corporate" look in favor of a warm, cozy, and approachable essence.

## Aesthetic Direction
An Autumn Warm theme paired with a minimalistic layout. 
Colors include warm olives, soft creams, muted oranges, and subtle browns. 
It supports both light and dark modes.
Design follows WCAG guidelines for accessibility.
